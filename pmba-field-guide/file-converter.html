Good to know
  • This pulls out the text. Fonts, images and page layout aren't carried over.
  • Spreadsheets keep their rows and columns when converted to Excel or CSV. Tables in PDFs and Word docs are split into columns where the spacing makes it clear.
  • Scanned PDFs and photos use OCR. The first time, your browser downloads the OCR engine (about 10 MB), so give it a moment. Double-check numbers it reads.
  • Old .doc and .ppt files aren't supported — open them and Save As .docx / .pptx first.
  • PDF output uses a basic font, so unusual symbols may show as ?. Use Word output if you need them.

University of Utah PMBA — a shared tool for classmates. Files are processed entirely in your browser and never leave your computer; helper libraries (pdf.js, Tesseract OCR, Mammoth, SheetJS, JSZip, jsPDF) load from public CDNs.

Module 1 & 2 · Q1Conceptual Question

Which equation captures the fundamental relationship between a company's resources and how they're financed?

How to work it
  1. The fundamental accounting equation is Assets = Liabilities + Stockholders' Equity — every dollar of assets is financed either by owners (equity) or nonowners (liabilities).
  1. B. Assets = Liabilities + Stockholders' Equity

Module 1 & 2 · Q2Conceptual Question

A firm reports total assets of $650 million and total liabilities of $420 million. Which statement is accurate?

How to work it
  1. Stockholders' Equity = Assets − Liabilities = $650M − $420M = $230M — the owners' residual claim after creditors are satisfied.
  1. C. Owners hold a $230 million residual claim on the firm's assets.

  1. B. Issuing shares of common stock for cash

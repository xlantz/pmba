Final check on the ad (ADPLAN, Unit 8)

  • Attention: does the opening hold someone who could skip it?
  • Distinction: would it be mistaken for any other camera ad?
  • Positioning: right category, one strong benefit, a reason to believe.
  • Linkage: will people remember the brand and the benefit, not just the ad?
  • Amplification: could anyone read it negatively?
  • Net equity: does it fit the brand you chose (a major manufacturer or a start-up)?

A useful habit for the paper: for each major decision, state what you expected, what happened, and what the data showed you afterwards that you could have seen sooner.

Prep prompt. The Adobe DDOM paper is due one week after the last class, alongside the MARKSTRAT paper. The simulation itself isn’t documented on this site, so nothing here describes how it works; follow the simulation’s instructions and the assignment. These are course frameworks you can use to reflect on your decisions 1–8.
  • Customer journey (Units 1 and 5): which stage of the journey did each decision try to move customers through, and where was the bottleneck?
  • Customer lifetime value (Unit 4): did your choices favor acquiring customers or keeping them, and what would CLTV say about that trade-off?
  • Resource allocation (Units 2 and 7): did spending follow marginal returns across channels, or habit?
  • Measurement (Unit 8): which metrics told you a decision worked, and were they communication effects or sales effects?
  • Data-driven marketing (Unit 5): what customer data did you use at each step, and what data did you wish you had?

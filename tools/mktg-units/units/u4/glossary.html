Present value (PV)
PV = FV / (1 + i)n: what a future amount is worth today.
Future value (FV)
FV = PV · (1 + i)n; $1 at 5% is $1.05 in a year, $1.1025 in two.
Net present value (NPV)
Discounted future returns minus today’s outlay; $100 for $40/yr × 3 at 5% → about $8.93.
Customer lifetime value (CLTV)
Present value of a customer’s future margins minus the cost of acquiring them.
CLTV, known lifetime N
Σn=1..N Mn/(1+i)n − AC.
CLTV with retention
Σn≥1 Mn·rn/(1+i)n − AC; r = share of customers kept each period.
Most common CLTV formula
m·r / (1 + i − r) − AC: first margin end of period 1, churn possible in period 1.
Case CLTV formula
m / (1 + i − r) − AC: no churn before the first margin. 22/(1.004166 − .98) = $910.37 gross.
Retention rate r / churn
r = share retained each period; churn = 1 − r (2% churn → r = 0.98).
Acquisition cost (AC)
Paid at period 0; subtract it at the last step.
Contract-then-churn CLTV
24-month no-churn annuity ($501.47) + discounted 6%-churn tail ($309.02) − $370 = $440.49.
Monthly discount rate
5% per year ÷ 12 = 0.004166 per month.

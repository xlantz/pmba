Contribution margin (CM)
Manufacturer’s selling price minus variable cost: each unit’s contribution toward fixed costs.
Margin vs. markup
Margins are a % of price: retail = selling price ÷ (1 − margin). $6 at a 40% margin = $10, not $8.40.
Break-even point (BEP)
FC / CM: units needed to cover fixed costs.
Break-even selling price (BESP)
(FC / volume) + VC: the price that breaks even at a given volume.
Break-even on added fixed cost
Additional FC / CM (e.g. extra units an ad campaign must sell).
Break-even on added variable cost
FC / (CM − new per-unit cost) − FC / CM: new BEP minus old BEP.
Experience (learning) curve
Unit cost falls a fixed percent each time cumulative production doubles: cn = c1·n−a.
Learning exponent a
a = ln(c1/cn) / ln n; with 110 and 60 at one million, a = 0.0438 (about 3% per doubling).
Break-even with learning
The volume N where the sum of each unit’s (rising) contribution equals fixed cost.
S-shaped response function
Sales respond slowly, then with increasing returns, then diminishing returns as spend rises.
MR = MC
Optimal spend: one more dollar of spend brings one more dollar of sales, on the diminishing-returns part of the curve.
Marginal return
(MR − MC) / MC; spend until it reaches zero.
Budget allocation across the mix
Put the next dollar where marginal return is highest until marginal returns are equal across elements.
Positioning
Designing the offering and image to occupy a distinctive place relative to competitors in the target’s mind.
Perceptual map
Plot of brands (and segment ideal points) on the dimensions customers use to compare them.
Ideal point
Where a segment’s ideal product sits on the map; brands near it are well positioned for that segment.
Stuck in the middle
Decent on everything, best at nothing (Bufferin, JC Penney, family restaurants); buyers pick specialists.
Attribute-based mapping
Ratings on attributes (semantic differential) → factor analysis → plot brands on factor scores.
Similarity-based mapping (MDS)
Pairwise similarity ratings → multidimensional scaling; researcher picks and interprets dimensions.
Semantic differential
Rating scale between opposite adjectives, e.g. Slow 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 Fast.

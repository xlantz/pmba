Final check on the ad (ADPLAN, Unit 8)

  • Attention: does the opening hold someone who could skip it?
  • Distinction: would it be mistaken for any other camera ad?
  • Positioning: right category, one strong benefit, a reason to believe.
  • Linkage: will people remember the brand and the benefit, not just the ad?
  • Amplification: could anyone read it negatively?
  • Net equity: does it fit the brand you chose (a major manufacturer or a start-up)?
